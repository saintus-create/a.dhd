## Get in Touch

Have a question, a correction to suggest, or a study to contribute?

**Email:** hello@managedcaresguide.org

**Response time:** Within 48 hours.

## Submit a Correction

Please include:
1. Page and section where the error appears
2. What the current data shows
3. The correct data with a supporting source (DOI, FDA document, or guideline URL)

## Contribute a Study

To suggest a peer-reviewed study, provide:
- Full citation (authors, year, journal, DOI)
- Brief summary of relevance
- Source category: International Research, U.S. LDX Studies, or Clinical Guidelines

## Evidence Brief Requests

If you are a managed care organization, PBM, or health plan and need a detailed evidence brief for a specific formulary review, reach out with your organization name and clinical question.

## Privacy

Information submitted is used only to respond to your inquiry. No personal data is sold or shared.
